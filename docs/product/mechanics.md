# Surface mechanics

Fine-grained mechanism for the reading surface and the transport beneath it: the
loops that keep a live, many-agent stream readable, and the properties each one
preserves.

A **closed loop** returns state to a fixed point and lets the system
quiesce. A **one-way flow** moves through a pipeline or decision fan.

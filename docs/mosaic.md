# Mosaic card lineage

[Spice implementation profile](implementation.md)

The Mosaic shows prose, failure, sign-out, operator direction, compaction,
fresh-session, and image-bearing tool activity as cards, plus a divider for
each noteworthy lane ending. Turn starts, reasoning, outbox transports, and
imageless tool calls remain lifecycle evidence: they take no card slot and
render no announcement. History paging follows the source frontier rather than
card eligibility, so a page holding only non-card records still leads to older
prose.

A fresh vendor session renders a "started fresh session" divider shaped like a
compaction boundary. The divider is a transcript card anchored at the lane
tape's session-state record that reports no messages yet, so live delivery,
resident restarts, browser reloads, and history pages all place it at that same
record exactly once. An exact-session resume reports its earlier messages and
renders none, and a state record that reports no message count infers none.

A committed compaction, automatic or manual, is one card at the tape locus of
the completion announcement the resident captured, placed at the accounting
time that read it; the vendor's session file supplies nothing. The card names
the strategy the completion reported: `snapcompact` renders as “snapcompacted
context”, while another method precedes “compacted context” by name. The method
reads quieter than the label it prefixes: regular weight in the console's muted
base-flavor neutral, never an agent, lane, or Design accent. A completion with
no method keeps the plain “compacted context” label.

Older history loads when a placed member frontier nears the viewport, the
reader reaches the plane's trailing edge, or a member lacks composer prose.
Missing prose can demand successive offscreen pages. A plane with no cards yet,
such as a team whose lanes have not run since the resident started, pages from
each member's newest history; a card still measuring does not itself establish
viewport demand. Each frontier is requested once
unless a fresh viewport gesture follows a completed page. An unanswered page
is requested again after reconnect, even if its frontier has moved offscreen.

Compaction and lifecycle dividers stay full-width boundaries between
chronological epochs. A replayed epoch rests against its retained boundary,
carrying all older history with it; where incremental placement cannot keep
every boundary whole, the plane replays in full. A paged plane therefore matches
a fresh load of the same cards however its pages were batched, and a card that
retention evicts leaves no gap.
